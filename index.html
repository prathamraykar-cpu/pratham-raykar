What I do

Full spectrum
delivery.

01
Structured Discovery & Requirements Engineering
I run intake workshops, story mapping sessions, and workflow design reviews to capture requirements with precision. I own the full BA lifecycle — PRDs, functional specs, BPMN diagrams, DoR validation, and value-tagged user stories — ensuring every work item is ready before it reaches engineering.
02
Presales, Solution Design & Client Positioning
I support RFP/RFI responses, demo preparation, and bid management — translating complex technical solutions into clear client value. I lead discovery conversations, present architectural approaches, and set delivery expectations before engagement begins, building trust from day one.
03
Flow Governance & Predictable Delivery
I drive delivery predictability through Kanban-based flow governance — WIP limit management, cycle time tracking, CFD reviews, Monte Carlo forecasting, and blocker triage. I translate flow metrics into commitments stakeholders can trust, and escalate risks before they become delays.
04
Stakeholder Management & Delivery Reporting
I manage the full stakeholder communication lifecycle — MoMs, weekly progress reports, AMS reports, CR tracking, increment completion emails, and monthly delivery projections. I keep internal teams and external clients continuously aligned with accurate, timely, and margin-aware delivery visibility.
05
Solution Architecture & Cloud Advisory
I define high-level solution architectures across AWS, Azure, and GCP — balancing feasibility, scalability, and risk. I provide TCO inputs, cloud cost advisory, and DR strategy guidance to support informed business and technology decisions at both presales and execution stages.
06
AI-Native Workflows & Tooling
I build and operate AI-driven productivity systems — MCP integrations, Claude-based automation, and multi-platform tooling — to compress turnaround cycles and standardise delivery output. AI is a structural capability in how I work, not an afterthought.
